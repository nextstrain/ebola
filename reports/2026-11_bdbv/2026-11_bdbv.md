## Chains are persisting in Health Zones for long times

> This section relies a lot on the DTA and is probably overstating the truth

While we currently infer the outbreak origin to be in the (combined) Health Zones of 'Bunia|Mongbwalu|Rwampara',
the longest chains of continuous transmission within individual Health Zones are from Nizi ([@fig:chain-duration-kde]).
We infer two separate jumps from Bunia|Mongbwalu|Rwampara into Nizi in around April/May, and each of those chains
continues to be present within Nizi.



:::figure{#fig:chain-duration-kde}
```yaml gazette
image: chain-duration-kde.png
```
**The duration of within-Health-Zone transmission chains**
Circle size scales with the number of tips in a chain.
The vast majority of chains persist for less than 1 month and involve only one sampled genome.

:::

## Some mutations are artifactual


Nucleotide 5246 (corresponding to residue 262 in VP40) is
:trigger[extremely homoplasic on the tree]{target=fig:sequencing-artifacts update="{layout: rect}"}.
When :trigger[comparing this mutation against the sequencing protocol employed]{target=fig:sequencing-artifacts update="{layout: scatter}"}
it becomes clear that the `5246G` mutation is only present in two sequencing protocols, "Amplicon_Artic_pan-ebola/1000/v2.0.0" and "Amplicon_Artic_bdbv/400". 

:::figure{#fig:sequencing-artifacts}
```yaml gazette
component: auspice-wrapper.js
lightDom: true
data:
  main: ebola_bdbv_drc-uganda-2026_2026-11-05.json
opts:
  panels: ["tree"]
  query: 'c=gt-nuc_5246&gmax=11266&gmin=2489&l=scatter&scatterX=gt&scatterY=sequencingProtocol'
  heightMultiplier: 1.5
```
**Homoplasic mutations at nuc 5246 are due to sequencing protocol used**
:::
